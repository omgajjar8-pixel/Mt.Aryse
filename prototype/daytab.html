What to expect

A short day but mostly uphill. Oak and rhododendron forest first, then the trees thin out and you come up onto an open ridge. Camp tonight is on that ridge.

Tonight at Tilandi

Strong winds are common here. Put on your windproof layer before the sun goes down, and keep your water bottle inside the tent so it doesn't freeze.

Story nearby · unlocks on the ridge Why it's called Lohajung

Local legend says the goddess Parvati fought the demon Lohasur at your base village. "Jung" means battle.
