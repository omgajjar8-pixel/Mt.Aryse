On the trail, point your camera at the skyline and Mt. Aryse names each peak.

Good to know

Tilandi camp gets strong winds. Keep a windproof layer on top.
In peak winter, deep snow can block the lake and the top. The ridge views still make the trek.
Mobile network in most of Lohajung. Expect none on the trail.

Getting there

Reach Rishikesh the day before Day 1. One direct bus leaves Rishikesh for Lohajung at 5 am and arrives around 5 pm. You can also go by shared jeep via Karnaprayag and Debal, or from Kathgodam.

If something goes wrong

The safest way out from any camp is back down to Lohajung. The nearest health centre is the PHC at Dewal, about 30 km away. For anything serious, go to Rudraprayag or Gopeshwar.
